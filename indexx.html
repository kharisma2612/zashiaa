GOT QUESTIONS?

Everything you need to know before ordering your custom dress.

?

How are custom sizes taken?

We guide you step by step with simple bust, waist, hip, and length measurements so your dress fits comfortably.

?

How long does tailoring take?

Most custom dresses take around 7–14 working days depending on fabric details and embroidery or ribbon accents.

?

Do you ship across Indonesia?

Yes! Every order is packed neatly and safely with shipping options available across cities and regions.

?

Can I request color or style tweaks?

Of course. You can share your inspiration, preferred sleeve style, or soft pink shade before we begin sewing.

?

What about fabric care?

Gentle hand wash or delicate cycle in cold water and air drying in the shade keeps soft fabrics looking fresh.

?

Can I order for special events?

Yes, we craft dresses for graduations, parties, bridesmaid moments, and sweet everyday outfits.
